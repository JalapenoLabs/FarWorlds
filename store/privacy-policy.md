# Farworlds privacy policy

Effective 14 September 2026.

Farworlds is a browser extension that replaces the new tab page with a procedurally generated 3D world.

**What it collects**: nothing. Farworlds has no server, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

**What it stores on your device**: your settings and your logbook (coordinates, names and small rendered images of worlds you visited or saved) in the browser's local storage for the extension. This data never leaves your browser. Removing the extension deletes it.

**Optional browser permissions**: the search bar uses your browser's default search engine to run a query you typed; the query goes only to that engine. The most visited shortcuts widget, if you turn it on, reads your most visited sites and their icons from the browser to display them. Neither list is stored by Farworlds or sent anywhere.
